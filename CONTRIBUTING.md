# Contributing

Thanks for helping with research.

## Good contributions

- Correcting technical facts with reliable sources.
- Adding citations to public kernel, device-tree, or hardware documentation.
- Recording exact upstream source tags and commit SHAs.
- Improving reproducibility of emulator-only experiments.
- Documenting limitations and failed experiments clearly.

## Before opening a change

- Explain what the change establishes and what it does not establish.
- Include source links for device and kernel claims.
- Include exact source revisions for build reports.
- Keep proprietary Apple system files, firmware, signing materials, and personal data out of the repository.
- Do not add phone-flashing, partition-writing, or security-bypass instructions.
- Do not upload unverified binaries or label experimental artifacts as bootable.

## Reporting results

Use the evidence labels in [DEVICE_RESEARCH.md](docs/DEVICE_RESEARCH.md). A successful compile is not the same as a successful boot, and an emulator test is not evidence of Moto G60 hardware support.
